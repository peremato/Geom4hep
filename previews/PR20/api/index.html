Points, vectors and transformations

Geom4hep.Point3 — Type
Point3{T}(x, y, z)

A position in 3D space. Point3 - Point3 gives a Vector3, Point3 ± Vector3 a Point3.

source
Geom4hep.Vector3 — Type
Vector3{T}(x, y, z)

A displacement or direction in 3D space. Transformations rotate but do not translate vectors.

source
Geom4hep.Point2 — Type
Point2{T}(x, y)

A position in 2D space (used for computations in the transverse plane).

source
Geom4hep.Transformation3D — Type
Transformation3D{T}(dx, dy, dz)
Transformation3D{T}(dx, dy, dz, rotation::Rotation{3})
Transformation3D{T}(dx, dy, dz, rotx, roty, rotz)
Transformation3D{T}(rotation::RotMatrix3, translation::Vector3)
one(Transformation3D{T})

Placement of a daughter volume (or of a boolean operand) in the frame of its mother. Applied to a point of the mother, it gives the point in the frame of the daughter: t * p == R * (p - t) for a translation t = (dx, dy, dz) and rotation R, so Transformation3D{T}(dx, dy, dz) places the daughter at (dx, dy, dz). Rotations come from Rotations.jl (RotXYZ, RotZXZ, ...); the angles rotx, roty, rotz build a RotXYZ. p * t applies the inverse transformation, and t1 * t2 composes them.

Shapes

Geom4hep.AbstractShape — Type
AbstractShape{T<:AbstractFloat}

Supertype of all the shapes (solids). T is the floating point type of the shape parameters and of the points and directions it works with (Float64 or Float32). A shape is defined in its own frame; it is positioned in a mother volume by the transformation of its placement.

Every shape implements inside, distance_to_in, distance_to_out, safety_to_in, safety_to_out, normal, extent, capacity and surface, and the mesh functions used for drawing (coordinates, faces, mesh).

source
Geom4hep.Box — Type
Box{T}(x, y, z)

Box (as G4Box) centred at the origin, with half lengths x, y and z along the axes. box[i] gives the half length along axis i.

source
Geom4hep.Trd — Type
Trd{T}(x1, x2, y1, y2, z)

Trapezoid (as G4Trd) with half length z along the z axis and rectangular faces at -z and +z with half lengths (x1, y1) and (x2, y2).

source
Geom4hep.Trap — Type
Trap{T}(z, θ, ϕ, y1, x1, x2, α1, y2, x3, x4, α2)
Trap{T}(x1, x2, y1, y2, z)

General trapezoid (as G4Trap), with half length z. The faces at -z and +z are trapezoids with sides parallel to the x axis:

  • at -z: half length y1 along y, half lengths x1 and x2 of the sides at y = -y1 and y = +y1, and α1 the angle to the y axis of the line joining the centres of these sides;
  • at +z: the same with y2, x3, x4 and α2.

θ and ϕ are the polar and azimuthal angles of the line joining the centres of the two faces. The second form is a right trapezoid with rectangular faces of half lengths (x1, y1) at -z and (x2, y2) at +z.

source
Geom4hep.Tube — Type
Tube{T}(rmin, rmax, z, ϕ₀, Δϕ)

Tube or tube segment (as G4Tubs) along the z axis: inner and outer radii rmin and rmax (rmin = 0 for a solid cylinder), half length z, and the ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ] (0, 2π for a full tube).

source
Geom4hep.Cone — Type
Cone{T}(rmin1, rmax1, rmin2, rmax2, z, ϕ₀, Δϕ)

Cone or cone segment (as G4Cons) along the z axis, with half length z: inner and outer radii rmin1 and rmax1 at -z, rmin2 and rmax2 at +z, and the ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ].

source
Geom4hep.CutTube — Type
CutTube{T}(rmin, rmax, z, ϕ₀, Δϕ, bottom_normal::Vector3, top_normal::Vector3)
CutTube{T}(rmin, rmax, z, ϕ₀, Δϕ, θb, ϕb, θt, ϕt)

Tube segment cut by two planes (as G4CutTubs): a Tube with radii rmin, rmax and ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ] whose ends are the planes through (0, 0, -z) and (0, 0, z) with outward normals bottom_normal and top_normal, given as vectors or by their polar and azimuthal angles.

source
Geom4hep.Sphere — Type
Sphere{T}(rmin, rmax, ϕ₀=0, Δϕ=2π, θ₀=0, Δθ=π)

Spherical shell or section (as G4Sphere): inner and outer radii rmin and rmax, the ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ] and the polar range [θ₀, θ₀ + Δθ]. Sphere{T}(0, r) is a full ball (G4Orb).

source
Geom4hep.Torus — Type
Torus{T}(rmin, rmax, rtor, ϕ₀=0, Δϕ=2π)

Torus or torus segment (as G4Torus): the points at a distance between rmin and rmax from the circle of radius rtor in the xy plane (the swept radius), within the ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ].

source
Geom4hep.Polycone — Type
Polycone{T}(rmin::Vector, rmax::Vector, z::Vector, ϕ₀, Δϕ)

Polycone (as G4Polycone, z-planes form): a sequence of cone sections along z. z are the z planes (increasing) and rmin, rmax the inner and outer radii at each plane; the ϕ range is [ϕ₀, ϕ₀ + Δϕ].

source
Geom4hep.Polyhedra — Type
Polyhedra{T}(nsides, rmin, rmax, z, ϕ₀=0, Δϕ=2π)

Polyhedra (as G4Polyhedra) with nsides sides in the ϕ range [ϕ₀, ϕ₀+Δϕ] and z planes z with inner and outer radii rmin and rmax. The radii are the distances from the z axis to the sides.

source
Geom4hep.BooleanUnion — Type
BooleanUnion(left, right, placement=one(Transformation3D{T}))
BooleanSubtraction(left, right, placement=one(Transformation3D{T}))
BooleanIntersection(left, right, placement=one(Transformation3D{T}))

Boolean combination of two shapes (as G4UnionSolid, G4SubtractionSolid and G4IntersectionSolid): the points in left or right, in left and not in right, or in both. right is placed in the frame of left with placement (as in place_daughter!). The operands can themselves be boolean shapes.

Shape functions

Geom4hep.inside — Function
inside(shape, point) -> kInside | kSurface | kOutside

Classify point (in the frame of shape) as inside the shape, on its surface (within tolerance) or outside it. The constants kInside, kSurface and kOutside are exported.

source
Geom4hep.distance_to_in — Function
distance_to_in(shape, point, dir)

Distance along the unit direction dir from point, outside shape, to where the ray enters the shape. Inf if the ray misses the shape. A point on the surface moving into the shape gets 0. The result is not meaningful for points inside the shape (see inside).

source
Geom4hep.distance_to_out — Function
distance_to_out(shape, point, dir)

Distance along the unit direction dir from point, inside shape, to where the ray leaves the shape. A point on the surface moving out of the shape gets 0, and a point outside the shape gets a negative value (-1).

source
Geom4hep.safety_to_in — Function
safety_to_in(shape, point)

Lower bound of the distance from point, outside shape, to the shape (an isotropic safe step). It is cheaper to compute than the exact distance; it is ≤ 0 for points inside the shape.

source
Geom4hep.safety_to_out — Function
safety_to_out(shape, point)

Lower bound of the distance from point, inside shape, to its surface (an isotropic safe step). It is ≤ 0 for points outside the shape.

source
Geom4hep.normal — Function
normal(shape, point)

Unit outward normal of shape at point. On edges and corners the normals of the surfaces meeting there are averaged. Returns the zero vector if point is not on the surface (within tolerance).

source
Geom4hep.extent — Function
extent(shape) -> (lower::Point3, upper::Point3)

Corners of an axis-aligned box, in the frame of the shape, that contains it.

source
Geom4hep.capacity — Function
capacity(shape)

Volume of the shape. For shapes without a closed formula it is estimated by sampling (see estimate_capacity).

source
Geom4hep.surface — Function
surface(shape)

Area of the surface of the shape. For shapes without a closed formula it is estimated by sampling (see estimate_surface).

source
Geom4hep.estimate_capacity — Function
estimate_capacity(shape; npoints=1_000_000)

Volume of shape estimated from the fraction of npoints sample points of its bounding box that are inside it. Typical accuracy with the default number of points is better than 0.1%.

source
Geom4hep.estimate_surface — Function
estimate_surface(shape; npoints=1_000_000)

Surface area of shape estimated by sampling segments of length δ with isotropic directions in its (enlarged) bounding box of volume V: the probability that a segment crosses the surface is A δ / 2V. Typical accuracy with the default number of points is about 1% (slightly underestimated).

source
Geom4hep.tolerance — Function
tolerance(T=Float64)

Thickness of the surfaces of the shapes: a point closer than tolerance(T)/2 to a surface is on the surface (inside returns kSurface). 1 nm for Float64 and 1 µm for Float32 (lengths in mm).

source
Geom4hep.push_tolerance — Function
push_tolerance(T=Float64)

Distance by which the navigator pushes a point beyond a boundary it has reached, to locate it in the next volume.

source

Volumes

Geom4hep.Volume — Type
Volume{T}(label, shape, material)

A logical volume (as G4LogicalVolume): a shape filled with a material, with daughter volumes placed inside it with place_daughter!. The same volume can be placed several times. vol[i] gives the volume of the i-th daughter, and vol[i, j, ...] goes down the hierarchy.

source
Geom4hep.PlacedVolume — Type
PlacedVolume{T}

A daughter volume placed in its mother: the volume and its transformation (see place_daughter!). The daughters of a Volume are in its daughters vector.

source
Geom4hep.place_daughter! — Function
place_daughter!(mother::Volume, placement::Transformation3D, daughter::Volume)

Place daughter inside mother with placement (see Transformation3D: Transformation3D{T}(x, y, z) places the daughter at (x, y, z) of the mother). The daughter must be contained in the mother and must not overlap the other daughters (this is not checked). Placing an Assembly places each of its volumes. Returns mother.

source
Geom4hep.Assembly — Type
Assembly{T}(label)

A group of volumes without a shape of its own (as G4AssemblyVolume): its daughters are placed directly in the mother when the assembly is placed with place_daughter!.

source
Geom4hep.world_volume — Function
world_volume(vol::Volume)

vol if its label is "world"; otherwise a new world volume (a box of vacuum 5% larger than vol) with vol placed in it.

source
Geom4hep.read_gdml — Function
read_gdml(filename, T=Float64) -> Volume{T}

Read a GDML file and return its world volume, with the shapes, materials and volume hierarchy it defines, with floating point type T. Lengths are converted to mm and angles to radians. Solids that are not supported are replaced by NoShape with a warning.

source

Geometry model and navigation

Geom4hep.close_geometry — Function
close_geometry(world::Volume; bvh_threshold=4, with_index=false)

Build a flat GeometryModel from a hierarchy of volumes. Boolean shapes are converted to CSG programs over primitive shapes, and a BVH is built for the daughters of volumes with more than bvh_threshold daughters.

With with_index=true it returns (model, index), where index.volumes[i] is the Volume stored as volume i of the model (the volume field of a NavState) and index.materials[i] the Material with index i (see material_index). This allows to attach information to the volumes and materials of the model, e.g. by name.

source
Geom4hep.GeometryModel — Type
GeometryModel

Flat representation of a geometry, built with close_geometry. D is 1 if the model has boolean (CSG) shapes, 0 otherwise. Use Adapt.adapt(backend, model) to move it to a GPU.

source
Geom4hep.ShapeRef — Type

Reference to a shape of a GeometryModel: kind (index in the tuple of shape arrays) and index.

source
Geom4hep.shape_call — Function
shape_call(f, model::GeometryModel, ref::ShapeRef, args...)

Call the shape function f (e.g. inside, distance_to_in, extent) on the shape ref of model without dynamic dispatch: shape_call(inside, model, model.volumes[1].shape, point).

source
Geom4hep.NavState — Type
NavState{T}

Position of a point in the volume hierarchy of a GeometryModel: the current volume, the path of placements from the world (depth levels) and the transformation from the global frame to the frame of the current volume. volume == 0 when the point is outside the world. blocked is the daughter (placement) of the current volume that has just been left, if any (see compute_step).

source
Geom4hep.locate — Function
locate(model::GeometryModel, point) -> NavState

Find the deepest volume containing the (global) point.

source
Geom4hep.compute_step — Function
compute_step(model::GeometryModel, state, point, dir, step_limit) -> (step, new_state)

Distance from the global point along dir to the next boundary (or step_limit), and the state after crossing it. The new state is computed for the point pushed slightly beyond the boundary.

source
Geom4hep.in_world — Function
in_world(state::NavState)

true if the point of state is inside the world volume.

source
Geom4hep.material_index — Function
material_index(model::GeometryModel, state::NavState)

Index in model.materials of the material of the current volume of state.

source
Geom4hep.xray — Function
xray(model::GeometryModel, axis, npixels; backend=CPU(), maxsteps=10_000, launch_time=0.5)

X-ray image of the world volume of model with rays parallel to axis (1, 2 or 3 for x, y, z): the mass thickness (density × length) integrated along each ray. npixels is the number of pixels along the first transverse axis. Returns (u, v, image, nsteps) with the pixel centres along the two transverse axes, the image and the number of navigation steps per pixel.

backend is a KernelAbstractions backend: CPU() (Julia threads), or a GPU backend such as MetalBackend() or CUDABackend() (the model is copied to the device). On GPUs the image is computed in several kernel launches, each one taking about launch_time seconds: a long-running kernel on a GPU that also drives a display is aborted by the operating system (e.g. macOS error "Impacting Interactivity").

Benchmarks

Geom4hep.ShapeBenchmark — Type
ShapeBenchmark{T}(name, shape, world, placement, npoints, nrep, vecgeom)

A benchmark case: a shape placed with placement (a Transformation3D as given to place_daughter!: Transformation3D(x, y, z) puts the shape at (x, y, z)) in a world box of half lengths world, timed for npoints points and nrep repetitions. vecgeom is the VecGeom benchmark program (and options) with the same setup.

source
Geom4hep.benchmark_shape — Function
benchmark_shape(shape; world, placement, npoints=1024, nrep=4, inside_bias=0.5, toin_bias=0.8,
                modes=(:shape, :model), seed=1, verbose=true)
benchmark_shape(b::ShapeBenchmark; kwargs...)

Time the shape functions as the shape benchmarks of VecGeom: inside, contains, distance_to_in, safety_to_in, distance_to_out and safety_to_out (see the header of Benchmark.jl). world are the half lengths of the world box (default: 4 times the extent of the shape) and placement the placement of the shape in it (as for place_daughter!). Returns a vector of named tuples (case, T, mode, op, npoints, nrep, seconds, ns), with the total time and the time per call in ns.

source
Geom4hep.vecgeom_suite — Function
vecgeom_suite(T=Float64)

The shape benchmarks of VecGeom (test/benchmark/*Benchmark.cpp) for the shapes of Geom4hep, with the same shape parameters, world box, placement, number of points and repetitions as the defaults of the VecGeom programs. Not reproduced: the corner-defined Trapezoid (type 1) and the (r,z) Polycone (type 4).
